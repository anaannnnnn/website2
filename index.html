    How TerraKit works

    1. Site — search or click the map.
    2. Frame — choose sheet + scale; rotate the frame to the street grid. Right-drag (or two-finger twist) rotates the map, Ctrl-drag tilts it.
    3. Data — terrain, buildings (Overture Maps + OpenStreetMap), streets, water, land use, trees and climate are fetched live. Each card shows the source and how reliable it is.
    4. Sections — A–A′ and B–B′ are added for you; draw more on the map and watch the profile update.
    5. Drawings — true-scale PDF/SVG sheets, CAD files, 3D models and laser-cut layers.

    Nothing is invented: when a building has no recorded height the drawings mark it, and missing data is reported, not filled in.
