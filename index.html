01 — About the group

Built on vision

Desire Group was founded to innovate, inspire and impact — a diversified business conglomerate shaping the future through excellence across multiple industries. Our journey began with a simple idea: to build brands that stand for trust, innovation and value.

From fresh groceries to precision healthcare, authentic dining to modern grooming and creative media — one group, seven brands, a single purpose: to deliver quality experiences that enrich everyday life.
